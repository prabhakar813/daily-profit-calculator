Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Welcome to Daily Profit Calculator. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Store

Daily Profit may store information you enter, including daily earnings, expenses, savings goals and profit records.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

The app uses your browser's local storage to save your records on your device. You do not need to create an account to use the calculator.

Your records may be lost if you clear browser or app data, uninstall the app, or reset your device. Please keep regular backups.

3. Backup and Restore

You can download a backup file containing your expenses, savings and daily records. You control where this file is saved and who can access it. Restoring a backup replaces the existing records in the app.

4. Data Sharing

The app does not require you to submit your financial records to create an account. Do not share your backup files with anyone you do not trust.

5. Third-Party Services

The app is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process standard technical information when you visit the website, according to its own privacy policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Daily Profit is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or data practices change.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the app publisher through the Daily Profit GitHub repository .

Thank you for using Daily Profit.
